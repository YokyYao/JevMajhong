The active curriculum still requires a complete Stage4 run, its original development assessment, and an independent final comparison against frozen v8. The source audit is a prerequisite for sealing that final comparison; it is not evidence that the model has improved.

The finite dispatch job waits for the existing Stage4 submission job. It then reads the actual register/integration/prepare/fit/audit/capture job IDs from that launcher's queue receipt, verifies the actual scheduler jobs, and submits the already preflighted final source producer. No future training job ID is invented in advance.

The producer depends on successful Stage4 audit and terminal capture, the complete targeted108 source observer, and the complete training-data origin proof. The producer itself still requires the actual final budget, original passing development assessment, and independently audited final checkpoint. A failed prerequisite does not admit a candidate or open final-test games.

New proof and terminal-capture jobs are created while held. Their actual ownership, entry scripts, and dependencies are checked before the queue receipt is written and the jobs are released. A failed submission cancels this dispatch's partial job chain. Source identities are frozen; completed or partial evidence is never overwritten. Each job has a GPU allocation, a fixed wall-time limit, logs, an exit receipt, and no requeue.
