automatic figures show loss, evaluation reward, hand-win/first/top-two rates.

net-point plotter only accepts the previous 3A objective. Do not edit that

and uses their actual stage names. It requires a completed2400-update fit,
175190 exposures and successful independent execution audit, including the
23688 reloaded menus,376 optimizer states and320 native ledgers. The original
strategy advancement result is retained even if it failed. A successful
export is not a successful candidate admission or independent confirmation.

verified subset of the final64 grid. The separate final64/eight-seed panel

source hashes and the original grade in the plot provenance. Export PNG/PDF;
do not smooth, invent observations, run more games or modify weights.
