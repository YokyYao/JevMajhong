# Prospective four-family actor-free source-reuse preflight

The terminal corpus has only8 independent TRAIN small-win proxy decisions.
Before collecting new source games or running Mortal again, test whether the
completed256 basic-course trajectories can supply additional unlabelled late
windows. This is an input/replay proof, not a teacher, target or student fit.

Use the original source registration, forced-win behavior, engine0.4.11,
spaced seed mapping and exact family splits. Predeclare offsets0/4/5/9: two
TRAIN and two validation families. No source outcome or resulting label is
used to select those families. Do not open the final32 seeds or alter active
calls data, parameters, loss, routing or budget.

Native clone trials reconstruct each next complete recorded MJAI prefix.
Take all offered legal wins. During non-winning response batches, canonical
PASS plus at most the one executed call can reproduce the output because
losing claim intentions are absent from MJAI. They remain explicitly unknown:
never export these reconstructed actions as expert accept/decline labels.
An unsupported/mismatched prefix fails rather than inventing a source action.
Require exact complete events, final scores and financial ledgers. Compare
all saved basic-teacher public observations/menus in those four families at
their original physical step/seat coordinates. Actual source labels are not
used to choose replay actions. Tests cover alternate discarded tiles, native
clone isolation, forced legal ron and rejection of logged win declines.

Before examining the source choice or result, capture at most one physical
window per family/category in scheduled East4/extensions: progression draw,
post-call discard, call response and legal win. Any active seat is eligible;
absolute-seat order breaks simultaneous ties. Keep non-win categories and
natural absent categories; do not restrict the course to win/pass examples.
For each captured window, replay the same current-hand physical actions into
the existing five public score contexts and require all non-score public
inputs/menus to remain identical. Variants retain the original family split;
five score views are not five independent examples. All generated inputs and
traces stay private and unlabelled.

Run four explicit native integration checks and this bounded audit in a
15-minute1-GPU/4-CPU allocation, with device/node checks, timeout, log and shell
exit. Bind source/config/native artifacts before execution; verify all active
fit dependencies remain unchanged. Do no model inference, new source play,
student update or label admission. A successful preflight permits preparing a
separately frozen full256-family harvest with fresh outputs; it does not itself
launch that job or prove terminal teacher reliability. Broader-family overlap,
tokenization/native reconstruction, scoring support and label sensitivity
still need their own admission checks before training.
