scores/dealer/wind/bank and physical seed to replay its saved native actions.

initial hands/draws, against the independent source MJAI slice before the
declared event offset. Rebuild the public request/menu and verify the fixed
single-actor own-draw, no-meld/no-RI/discards-only scope.

Find the label independently from the original explicit dahai event, matching
actor and normalized tile identity to exactly one current legal candidate.
Do not trust the collector's chosen-action receipt as the label oracle, and
never create expert negatives from absent/suppressed response intentions.
Verify all original family splits/IDs and source seeds, all675 reserved groups,
and available cleaned/calls/risk teacher opposite-split inputs/groups; reject
new same-split duplicates rather than silently multiplying their gradient mass.

label unchanged and encode with the fixed tokenizer/limits/legal-menu order.
Retain and independently recompute old guard exclusions, including no-threat
shanten-increase targets. Those examples are not silently dropped or relabelled;
any new training recipe needs a compatible admitted execution route. Report
turn-slot and eligibility counts, distinct families and actual token maxima.

admission or strength claim. Publish code/plan and aggregate outcomes only;
raw observations, labels, source offsets and traces stay private.
