# Final placement assessment and retained early decisions

Freeze `configs/training/curriculum-placement-assessment-v1.json` in the launch
binding before the first full-course update. The budget registration alone
cannot supply this permission. Intermediate noise does not end training;
the candidate is always the registered final checkpoint after two epochs.

Development assessment compares the changed child policy's fresh64-game zero
baseline with its final64 games on the same eight logical seeds, four seats
and two opponent profiles. Top-two point gain must be nonnegative. Net points,
first/fourth, hand-win and dealin are reported alongside paired seed intervals.
Fourth place may take risks: there is no conditional fourth-escape veto or
development net-point veto. These choices do not change the final goal, which
still requires an untouched independent comparison with v8 on BOTH top-two and
net points. Eight consumed development seed groups cannot prove that claim.

To check retained capabilities without treating terminal strategy changes as
errors, use every original curriculum2 hard-labelled validation record before
East4/extensions. The actual serving rule retains early forced wins and the
fixed full-class RI0.5 selector. For legacy and teacher basic sources, separately
limit ordinary-label agreement and RI balanced-accuracy drops to3 percentage
points. Limit mean balanced-accuracy loss over chi, pon, ankan, daiminkan and
kakan to3 points. The same cases/class denominators must be used at both
endpoints, with both operation classes present. Missing classes are unassessed,
not invented recall. These are retained-label proxies, not optimality claims.

`tools/audit_curriculum_placement_course_v1.py` independently reconstructs all
registered update/source counts and loss components, exact warm-start tensors,
early probability inheritance, full57-source validation profiles and final
serving reload probabilities. It checks the durable final376-state optimizer,
actual expanded dataset identity and full final epoch permutation. Minimum-CE
metadata is diagnostic; it cannot replace the final game candidate.

All actual development matches are checked against native settlement ledgers,
ranks, wins, dealins and operation events. Summaries and metric journals must
reconstruct exactly. The same16-cell subset supplies every plotted curve point;
