# Native kan-source pilot: actual collection, teacher labels pending

The new source collection has completed. It supplies legal opportunities for
later labeling, not examples already admitted to student training. The running
paired3A fit, its criteria, prepared3B recipe and published model are unchanged.

There are24 TRAIN and eight validation families. The192 prospective plans
link initial/effective hand shapes, suit/rank symmetries, all three operation
variants and two physical worlds before hash-based whole-family splitting.
Three TRAIN kakan prefixes that would decline a legal win were quarantined
without replacement. Every remaining source independently reproduced its
exact native prefix, public request and full legal menu. All136 physical
tiles occur exactly once in every constructed wall.

These deliberately biased early-hand contexts do not represent natural-game
frequencies or late defensive situations. A motif describes construction,
not the correct action. The explicit skip alternatives remain available.
Six TRAIN menus also offer a legal win; the teacher's existing forced-win
shortcut will be recorded separately rather than credited as learned kan
supervision. No positive-kan quota or teacher-action filter is applied.

A finite independent GPU task is actually queued to label all189 sources
with the fixed Mortal-298k actor after the current3A fit terminates. Its
source files and recipe were bound before execution. A fresh actor receives
only the actual redacted public prefix, with native own-hand and legal-action
checks. Source-prefix actions are not labels. Teacher choices, including
skips, must map uniquely to native physical candidates and be submitted to
the engine; simultaneous priority can override a submitted call intention.

At publication, **teacher labels have not been produced and student updates
on this cohort are zero**. Teacher agreement alone will not establish positive
action advantage. Conditional-world comparisons, full existing-corpus and
reserved-family overlap, normalization/encoder coverage, loss allocation and
a new training registration are still needed before using these sources in a
fit. The final independent comparison grid remains unopened.

Three source-family/wall/public-history checks passed inside the existing
GPU allocation's companion CPU task. The189 native replays are separate
actual generation evidence. The teacher network and its actual current
choices have not been exercised by those three checks. Execution has finite
limits, logs and exit receipts; no login computation or background worker is
used. Raw hands, walls, traces and labels remain private.

reproducer's actual live3A scheduler job; `--nodelist` is optional. It checks

submitting the dependent finite teacher job. Existing bindings and outputs
