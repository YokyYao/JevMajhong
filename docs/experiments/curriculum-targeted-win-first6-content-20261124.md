# Targeted placement labels: fixed first-six TRAIN content

A finite allocated-node analysis inspected the fixed first six completed
training views in their original preregistered order. These are six distinct
source families, all in one bucket: fourth place with a5000-point gap, with
current legal win and concealed-kan alternatives. This partial cohort is not
representative of all108 views, other contexts or validation data.

Both original16-world blocks and every current legal candidate were retained:
192 public worlds and2432 complete-match candidate arms. Native ledgers and
the original512-bootstrap soft targets were independently reconstructed.
The saved-data analysis took13.14 seconds, without loading a model or producing
new rollouts, labels or optimizer updates.

The pooled32-world point choice and modal bootstrap label have the same action
type in these six cases. This establishes varied training targets in this
bucket, including accepting and declining a legal win. It does not establish
that a trained student makes those decisions correctly.

Only3/6 cases have the same preferred candidate in the two independent
16-world blocks. In4/6, every leave-one-world check retains the pooled point
choice. These are different robustness checks and need not have identical
counts. Mean maximum soft-target mass is0.70696; mean normalized entropy
is0.31517. Bootstrap target mass is an uncertain training preference, not a
calibrated probability that the action is optimal or will win.

The collection continues with all original views, splits and worlds. No case
was selected, removed or relabelled based on this analysis. Full-cohort source
coverage and uncertainty still require assessment after collection. Preserve
the full soft distribution rather than treating these modal choices as expert
hard labels. These cases do not enter the frozen active3A fit; their placement
use still requires the full data pipeline and qualified later parent.

Candidate advantages measured on the same sampled worlds used to choose the
label are subject to selection bias. They are not independent student results
or evidence of improvement over v8. No final reserved evaluation was opened.
Raw sources, worlds, per-case diagnostics and targets remain private.
