pipeline now connects four completed original/recovered shards to the original
full108-view merge and coherent placement-data preparation. All original
source selections, partitions,32-world labels, soft targets and admission

population/teacher/count checks, partial-folder rejection and rejection of
changed recovery bindings or live predecessor evidence. The preflight also
checked that the actual current incomplete shard is rejected. The entry and
both original functions are now hash-bound; no merge/preparation job was
submitted or run by these checks.

and output directory. Existing coherent replay must remain object-exact;
new labels, targets, family splits and native encoder/menu admission remain
unchanged. Additional producer bindings retain the recovery and real terminal
ancestry alongside the original completion bindings.
