from576 source plans;22 prefix-generation quarantines remain excluded. Source

teacher eligibility and selected-action identities were checked for every case.
This is a data-source result, not a student-training or win-rate result.

## Consequences for training data

**The two positives remain validation data.** They are not moved, copied or
near-duplicated into TRAIN. The newly labelled TRAIN portion still supplies
zero daiminkan positives. Blindly appending its395 passes would not repair the
missing teacher-source positive coverage, so this cohort remains separate from
the frozen active trial. Global corpus-family normalization, encoder checks and
fit admission are still pending.

The existing TRAIN corpus already contains341 observed legacy daiminkan
positives. A separate fixed TRAIN panel found that the completed student
matches only5/64 sampled positives, compared with0/64 for its parent. Thus both
positive-label fitting and source coverage need attention; repeating all data
or forcing every legal kan is not supported by these results.

For a future revision, use TRAIN-only, predefined context strata and preserve
their source families. Compare daiminkan, pon and pass with paired public-world
outcomes where feasible, retaining uncertainty and untested-action masks.
Do not select new training families around the two observed validation calls
or use validation choice frequencies as a positive mining target. Teacher
actions and simulated preferences remain evidence with limits, not expert
optimality certificates.
